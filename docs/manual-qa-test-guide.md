## 9. Supplier Payments & Allocation Engine

### Test ID: SPAY-01
**Module:** Supplier Payments
**Feature:** GENERAL Credit Allocation and Leakage Prevention
**Business Rule:** GENERAL credit automatically allocates to oldest purchases. SPECIFIC credit does NOT leak to other purchases.

**Steps:**
1. Create Purchase A for `Supplier Delta`, Total `500`.
2. Create a `SPECIFIC` Supplier Payment of `500` against Purchase A.
3. Edit Purchase A: reduce quantity so Total is now `300`. (This leaves 200 of the SPECIFIC payment unallocated).
4. Create Purchase B for `Supplier Delta`, Total `400`.
5. Check the Payment Status of Purchase B.

**Expected Result:**
Purchase B remains `UNPAID` (Allocated: 0). The 200 leftover from the `SPECIFIC` payment must NOT automatically pay Purchase B. 
*(If you repeat this test with a GENERAL payment, the leftover 200 WILL automatically apply to Purchase B).*
