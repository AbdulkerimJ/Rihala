# Rihala Vision

## Purpose

Rihala is a workshop management system designed to help furniture manufacturing businesses manage their daily operations.

The goal is to replace paper-based records and scattered spreadsheets with a centralized system that tracks customer orders, workers, purchases, operating expenses, and business performance.

The system should simplify business operations while remaining easy to use for workshop owners and employees.

---

## Long-term Vision

Rihala aims to become a complete ERP-like solution for furniture manufacturers.

Future versions may include:

- Inventory Management
- Production Tracking
- Customer CRM
- Supplier Management
- Mobile App
- Reports & Analytics
- AI-powered Insights
